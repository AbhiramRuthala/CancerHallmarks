ONCOLAB / FIELD GUIDE

The biology

THE BIOLOGY BEHIND THE SWITCHES

Common routes, not a fixed sequence.

Cancers evolve through different combinations of growth, survival and escape capabilities. There is no universal order or single “most activated” hallmark.

Frequently altered: growth signaling through RTK–RAS–MAPK and PI3K–AKT, plus disrupted p53 and RB/cell-cycle control, recur across cancer types. Growth signals may be activated; protective brakes are often lost. Frequency depends on tumor subtype. TCGA pathway study ↗

Why treatment is difficult: restoring a lost brake is a different challenge from blocking an overactive signal. Tumors can use alternative signaling routes, evade immune attack or select resistant clones. Invasion and distant spread add different tissue environments and treatment barriers. There is no universal “hardest” hallmark. Resistance ↗ · Metastasis ↗

Live hallmark amplification ⓘ

Each readout compares this run with a parallel run where that hallmark was never enabled. All other controls and their timing match. Positive values mean more population; negative values mean less. Treatment can reverse an advantage.

Effects overlap and must not be added together. They are model comparisons, not clinical effect sizes. pp = percentage points. Below one comparison population unit, the display uses absolute units instead of an unstable percentage. Reset clears all comparisons.

Reading hallmark amplification

Eight parallel counterfactual runs begin from the same population. Each permanently disables one hallmark and follows every other intervention at the same simulated time. Population change is (actual − counterfactual) / counterfactual × 100%. Below one counterfactual unit, an absolute difference replaces the percentage. Vessel supply and invasion differences use percentage points; genome instability also shows the difference in absolute resistant units. Effects are interacting, history-dependent and non-additive; they are not empirical causal estimates. A hallmark switched off after use can retain a historical effect. Reset and presets reset all comparisons.

Stage is not aggressiveness

This simulation cannot assign stage. It lacks clinical dimensions, anatomical depth, histology, lymph nodes and distant organs. Neither fast growth nor many hallmarks implies stage IV. A treatment response does not automatically rewrite the stage assigned at diagnosis.

What the math means

Sixteen population compartments track every combination of resistance to four therapies. Division is resource-limited; death includes baseline turnover, immune attack and treatment. New resistance arises during division. Treatment suppresses sensitive compartments more strongly, allowing resistant variants to be selected. No treatment invents resistance on demand.

Growth = division × (1 − population / capacity), bounded below at zero. Capacity follows vessel supply; growth-suppressor evasion raises capacity. Immortality removes a time-dependent replication limit. Mutation increases transitions between resistance compartments. Each resistance trait carries a small growth disadvantage through extra turnover.

Targeted therapy represents a matched growth-pathway inhibitor: it is effective primarily when sustained proliferation is enabled. Immunotherapy represents checkpoint-like restoration of immune killing. Chemotherapy favors dividing populations. Radiation is oxygen-dependent and modeled as continuous pressure, not clinical fractions. Combination effects are additive in killing rates; interactions and toxicity are omitted.

Read the display carefully

Dots are a deterministic visual sample of compartment populations, not simulated individual biological cells. Blood vessels and immune dots visualize model indices; vessel geometry and immune-cell collisions do not cause killing. Fractional population units persist: this model cannot declare cure. The invasion index is local spread, not metastasis. Runs stop at t = 300.

Limits & sources

All numbers and time units are chosen for classroom visibility, not fitted to experiments. One fixed, generic environment is used throughout; this model does not represent a specific organ or cancer subtype. Hallmarks interact in real tumors; these switches simplify that biology. This is a selected eight-capability teaching set, not an exhaustive hallmark taxonomy. No diagnostic, prognostic, dosing or treatment-planning use.
